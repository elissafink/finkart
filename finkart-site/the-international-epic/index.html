The International EPIC

The Founders of the International EPIC

Leroy G. Black
William G. Fink

“I look to my left, and I look to my right, and I still do not understand what life is about” … Bill Fink

Kindness, art and experimenting help us better understand ourselves and the world around us.

The goal of The International EPIC is to create The Experimental Photo Image Center that will eventually present cutting-edge artists who merge art together with science to make interesting and thought-provoking artwork.

The SEEDS Foundation is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit working toward creating “The Experimental Photo Image Center”. If you would like to help with PR, marketing or special art projects, please let us know.
