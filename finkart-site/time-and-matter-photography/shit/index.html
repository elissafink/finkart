Shit

Pictures of people who are: vicious · vile · wicked · peresh · predators · abusers · skubala · murderers

SHIT is defined as a contemptible or a worthless person.

It is up to our individual interpretation to determine what shit can represent. Pictures made of different kinds of shit present a valid way of defining and expressing human disgust from mistreatment, torment & hate. People like Hitler, Pol Pot, Stalin, Mao Zedong, Trotsky, Lenin, dictators, terrorists, sadistic politicians, inhuman murderers, sexual predators, and the torment of war and human struggle can be represented by a shit picture.
